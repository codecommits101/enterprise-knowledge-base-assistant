# Corporate Employee Benefits and Total Compensation Handbook (2024–2025)

## 1. Health, Dental, and Vision Coverage
The company offers comprehensive health insurance starting on the first day of the calendar month following the date of employment:
- **Medical Plans**: High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) with Health Savings Account (HSA) contribution, or Preferred Provider Organization (PPO) Plan.
- **HSA Contribution**: The company contributes $1,200 annually for individual coverage and $2,400 for family coverage, deposited in quarterly installments.
- **Dental & Vision**: 100% employer-covered preventive care, with orthodontia and annual corrective lens allowances up to $350.

## 2. Retirement & 401(k) Matching Program
- **Provider**: Fidelity Investments.
- **Employer Match**: The company matches 100% of employee contributions up to the first 4% of eligible base salary, and 50% on the next 2% of salary (maximum total company match of 5%).
- **Vesting Schedule**: Immediate 100% vesting on all employee contributions and employer matching funds from day one.

## 3. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave of Absence
- **Flexible Vacation (Salaried Exempt)**: Unlimited responsible PTO subject to manager coordination and meeting project deliverables.
- **Accrued PTO (Non-Exempt Hourly)**: 20 days (160 hours) accrued annually during the first 3 years of service, increasing to 25 days after year 3.
- **Company Holidays**: 12 paid federal holidays plus 2 floating personal days per calendar year.
- **Paid Parental Leave**: 16 weeks of 100% paid leave for all primary and secondary caregivers following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.

## 4. Professional Development & Education Reimbursement
- **Tuition & Certification Stipend**: Up to $3,500 USD per calendar year for accredited university degree coursework, AWS/GCP/Azure cloud certifications, or specialized technical bootcamps.
- **Annual Conference Budget**: Up to $1,500 USD annually for conference registration, travel, and lodging with manager endorsement.
