# Global Enterprise Remote Work & Hybrid Operations Policy (v4.2)

## 1. Overview and Eligibility
This document outlines the official corporate policy regarding flexible and remote work arrangements. All full-time and part-time salaried employees who have completed their initial 90-day probationary period are eligible to apply for remote or hybrid status, subject to departmental approval.

## 2. Work Classifications
- **Fully Remote**: Employees working 100% off-site within an approved geographical tax jurisdiction.
- **Hybrid**: Employees required to work from a designated regional hub office a minimum of 2 days per week (typically Tuesdays and Thursdays for collaborative syncs).
- **On-Site Essential**: Roles requiring physical access to hardware labs, secure data centers, or executive facilities.

## 3. Home Office Equipment Allowance & Stipend
To support ergonomic and productive home working environments, the company provides:
- **One-time Initial Setup Allowance**: Up to $1,000 USD upon hire for desk, ergonomic chair, monitor, docking station, and peripherals. Reimbursed via standard Concur expense reporting with itemized receipts.
- **Monthly Internet & Utility Subsidy**: $75 USD per month added directly to payroll for high-speed broadband connectivity.
- **Standard Issue Hardware**: Enterprise MacBook Pro 16" (M-series) or Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon, dual 27-inch 4K monitors, and encrypted hardware security keys (YubiKey 5C).

## 4. Working Hours and Availability
- Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in the employee's primary regional time zone (EST, CST, PST, GMT, CET).
- Status on Slack and Microsoft Teams must accurately reflect working status (Active, In a Meeting, Focus Time, Away).
- Out-of-Office (OOO) must be set on Google Calendar with a minimum of 48 hours advance notice for scheduled PTO.

## 5. Travel and Relocation Policy
Employees wishing to work from an alternate state or international country for more than 14 consecutive calendar days must submit an International Remote Work Approval Form to HR and Legal at least 30 days prior to travel to ensure compliance with international labor laws and tax withholdings.
